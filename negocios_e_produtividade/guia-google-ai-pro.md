> **Objetivo:** Coleção completa de prompts práticos aprendidos no curso Google AI Pro, focados em produtividade, design, análise de dados e negócios.

Este documento é um compilado de 15 fluxos de trabalho avançados utilizando IA (Gemini/ChatGPT). Cada fluxo é dividido em etapas para garantir o melhor resultado possível. 

Copie os blocos de texto abaixo e substitua os valores entre `[colchetes]` pelas suas informações.

---

### 1. Diretrizes de Design e Feedback Criativo
**Etapa 1: Obter diretrizes de design**
```text
What are some guidelines that should be followed when it comes to designing [type of creative asset, e.g. flyers] for a [type of organization, e.g. IT consulting business]?
```
**Etapa 2: Obter feedback específico sobre seu ativo** (Anexe sua imagem)
```text
Review this [type of creative asset, e.g. flyer] for a [type of organization, e.g. an IT consulting business]. The goal is to [primary goal of the asset, e.g. get more leads from small business owners]. Provide constructive feedback based on the guidelines above. Highlight weaknesses and suggest specific improvements.
```
**Etapa 3: Rascunhar uma nota de feedback**
```text
Write a short note to share the feedback recommendations with the [role of the person receiving the feedback, e.g. designer of this flyer].
```

---

### 2. Plano de Medição e Métricas
**Etapa 1: Identificar as principais métricas da apresentação**
```text
I work at an [your company type, e.g. apparel company]. I'm preparing for an [type of meeting, e.g. executive review of website performance]. Our goal is to [primary business goal, e.g. increase revenue in Q3]. What are the top 5 metrics I should consider for my presentation?
```
**Etapa 2: Antecipar perguntas da liderança**
```text
What questions might our leadership team ask about [one of AI's suggested metrics from the previous step]?
```

---

### 3. Criação de Aplicativo Personalizado (AI Studio)
**Etapa 1: Descreva o aplicativo que você deseja criar**
```text
I want to build an app that takes [inputs] and does [process] to create [output] that helps me [goal].
```
**Etapa 2: Iterar em seu aplicativo**
```text
Add [element, e.g. a button] that [purpose, e.g. copies all the content so I can paste it into a spreadsheet].
```
**Etapa 3: Alterar o estilo visual**
```text
Change the [visual property, e.g. the color scheme] of the app to use [style description, e.g. shades of blue].
```

---

### 4. Geração de Imagens Personalizadas
**Etapa 1: Brainstorming de conceitos criativos**
```text
I need some ideas for a [type of image, e.g. concert poster] about [what the image is about, e.g. a music band]. The style should be [visual style, e.g. modern, retro, or minimalist] and the mood should be [mood, e.g. excited, calm, or mysterious]. Please give me 3 different creative concepts. For each concept, provide a: Description: A brief explanation of the visual layout. Color Palette: A set of colors that fit the mood.
```
**Etapa 2: Gerar a imagem com base no conceito escolhido**
```text
Based on the [chosen creative concept] and the text '[insert your favorite headline]', generate a detailed image. The image should feature [subject] in a [visual style] style. Ensure the composition includes [layout requirements]. The overall mood should be [mood/vibe].
```
**Etapa 3: Refinamento**
```text
This is great, but can you move the [subject] to the [position] to leave more room for my text on the [opposite side]?
```

### 5. Extração e Limpeza de Dados (Google Sheets)
**Etapa 1: Extrair e formatar dados de uma imagem** (Anexe a imagem)
```text
Extract the [data you want, e.g. reviews] from this [source, e.g. screenshot] and turn them into a [data format, e.g. table] with the following columns [column headers, e.g. date, text, and # of stars].
```
**Etapa 2: Classificar dados usando fórmula (Exclusivo Google Sheets)**
```text
=AI("Analyze the [data to classify, e.g. customer feedback] and classify it into [category list, e.g. Positive, Neutral, or Negative]", [cell with customer feedback text, e.g. B2])
```

### 6. Avaliação de Risco de Projetos
**Etapa 1: Identificar dependências ausentes** (Anexe os documentos do projeto)
```text
You are a [persona, e.g. project manager]. My company is launching a [product, service or initiative]. I've attached [document 1, e.g. a launch memo] and [document 2, e.g. a launch work-back schedule]. Are there any dependencies missing from the plan?
```
**Etapa 2: Gerar plano de mitigação/linha do tempo**
```text
Create a timeline listing key milestones for [the missing dependency from the previous step]. Flag what might be missing from my current plan.
```

### 7. Criação e Edição de Slides de Apresentação
**Etapa 1: Gerar o esqueleto da apresentação** (Anexe o documento base)
```text
Acting as an expert [type of expertise you need, ex: designer] help me create a text-only slide deck based on [document you have supplied, ex: new refund policy].
```
**Etapa 2: Personalizar para diferentes públicos**
```text
Change the slide deck to be for [new type deck you need, ex: an internal onboarding deck for new hires]. Use only the information provided in the slides.
```

### 8. Resumo Inteligente de Reuniões
**Etapa 1: Gerar um relatório a partir de uma transcrição** (Anexe o texto)
```text
Acting as [a role that would excel at the task you want to accomplish, e.g. a senior project manager], generate a report based on the attached transcript. The report must have the following sections: [description of the sections you want in the report].
```

### 9. Relatório de Feedback Estratégico
**Etapa 1: Simular a perspectiva do Stakeholder** (Anexe a proposta)
```text
I'm working on a [type of document, e.g. proposal or report] for [brief description of your project]. Act as the [stakeholder you want feedback from and list their priorities], and review the document attached. From your perspective, is there any information missing?
```

---

### 10. Simulador de Negócios e Impacto
**Etapa 1: Identificar alavancas de negócios** (Anexe seus dados/resultados)
```text
I'm a [your role] at [place of work]. My main goal is to [goal, e.g. increase my profit this year]. Acting as a business advisor, analyze my [type of data] and give me a list of the 3 most important changes I can make that would have the biggest impact on my goal.
```
**Etapa 2: Criar simulador interativo**
```text
Based on those insights and my data, create an interactive [type of tool, e.g. profit calculator] for my [type of business]. Let me adjust [type of component e.g. toggles, sliders, switches] for each change to show their effect.
```

### 11. Planejamento Estratégico e Análise de Custo-Benefício
**Etapa 1: Brainstorming**
```text
You are a [persona, e.g. marketing expert] in the [industry]. My company is developing a [new product]. Help me brainstorm [items to brainstorm, e.g. accessories] that I could [purpose, e.g. bundle with this product].
```
**Etapa 2: Tabela de Análise**
```text
Perform a [type of analysis, e.g. cost-benefit analysis] for each of these ideas. Format your response as a table with the following columns: [Column 1 name, e.g. Idea] | [Column 2 name, e.g. Cost (low/medium/high)] | [Column 3 name, e.g. Benefit (low/medium/high)]
```

---

### 12. Análise e Visualização de Dados Narrativos
**Etapa 1: Resumo e extração de métricas** (Anexe a planilha)
```text
This is data from [source of your data]. Provide the [first metric you want, e.g. total purchase revenue] and [second metric you want].
```
**Etapa 2: Narrativa Analítica**
```text
You are an [persona]. For an [your business type], why might the [specific finding from Step 1]? Create a [chart type, e.g. bar chart] that shows [metric to visualize] by [data column for chart axis].
```

### 13. Conselhos de Especialistas (Criação de Personas)

```text
You are [expert role, e.g. highly competent interior designer]. Your key characteristics include: [characteristics]. I am a [your role]. Use your expertise to challenge my thinking and ensure my ideas are sound. Your advice should be clear, candid, and informative. Stay in character throughout the conversation.
```
